# ASHBOURNE

This is my cinematic universe project.

Ashbourne is a world built around emotional experiments and people developing abilities based on how they feel under pressure. It’s not just a story, it’s also connected to a streetwear brand idea I’m building (ASHBORN).

---

## What it is
Ashbourne is about a system that studied emotions and ended up creating subjects with different abilities because of it.

---

## Characters

**Kaethryn**  
Her power is tied to anger. When she loses control emotionally, things around her start reacting too.

**Nox**  
She takes in other people’s emotions, especially pain and sadness. It affects her a lot.

**Hazel**  
She can sense what people feel and influence emotions and attachment.

**Trey**  
He can copy abilities for short moments and slows things down around him when he’s active.

---

## Why I’m building this
I’m turning this into a full universe with visuals, characters, and eventually clothing designs under ASHBORN.
